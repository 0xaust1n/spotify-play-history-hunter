# Roadmap

## Spotify Persona

A dedicated page for showing a user's Spotify listening profile, such as top artists, top tracks, and track audio traits like energy, danceability, mood, tempo, and acousticness.

## Instant Spotify Wrapped

A dedicated page for creating a Spotify Wrapped-style summary for the current year or a previous year. The idea is to make Wrapped available anytime, without waiting for Spotify's annual release or generating official image and video assets.
